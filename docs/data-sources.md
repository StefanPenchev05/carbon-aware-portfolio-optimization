# Data acquisition checklist

No licensed research access was supplied. No real dataset was fetched, evaluated for suitability or verified. The following are candidate source categories to investigate with the university, not validated coverage or licensing claims.

- [ ] Obtain the selected STOXX Europe 600 universe and dated historical constituent snapshots from an authorized index-data source. Record effective dates, identifiers and license.
- [ ] Ask the university library about institutional financial and ESG databases (for example LSEG, Bloomberg, S&P or MSCI). Confirm actual access, export permission, sector coverage and publication restrictions before integration.
- [ ] Investigate issuer annual/sustainability reports for disclosed Scope 1, 2 and 3 values. Retain document references, page numbers, fiscal-year boundaries, publication dates, restatements and methodology.
- [ ] Select daily market prices with documented corporate-action adjustment, exchange calendars and currency. Confirm delisted-company availability and permitted storage/redistribution.
- [ ] Map ISINs, historical ticker/exchange changes, sector taxonomy and corporate events. Do not match on ticker alone.
- [ ] Record Scope 2 location/market basis, Scope 3 categories, organizational boundaries and reported units. Keep incompatible series separate.
- [ ] Capture provider retrieval time, data vintage, fiscal period, available/publication date, quality flags and license in a source manifest. Extend schema for publication and fiscal boundaries before point-in-time analysis.
- [ ] Audit missingness and cross-source discrepancies before defining research eligibility.
- [ ] Confirm whether derived results and raw extracts may be published with the dissertation and repository.

All current CSVs are self-authored synthetic examples for software demonstration only. They provide three fictional companies, seven fabricated price observations and three fabricated annual emissions records; one company has no emissions records. The invented ISIN-shaped identifiers are not verified securities. Synthetic data can be reused with this project and must retain its labeling. No claim of real STOXX membership is made.
